What turned up when we took it apart

A cloak that stops half the hits

In the action scenes each hit takes one point of life and you start with 20 (p11:A7C5). Carrying item 11, the cloak (マント), only one hit in two counts (p11:A7B8).

Read in the code.

The password is the whole game

The 62 bytes of the game (from 0xEB40) plus a CRC-16 (polynomial 0xA001), all mixed with a key: 130 letters (p14:BAAC). If the CRC does not match, it is not loaded (p14:BCEA).

Read in the code.

It saves to another cartridge

Besides tape and disk, it looks in the slots for a cartridge with “YZ” at 0x4010 and talks to it through 0x8000 (p01:60CB).

In the story, the hero is you

The story sets “あなた” (“you”) as the first name before introducing the characters (p04:6E5D).

The bosses are drawn with tiles

Seven of the nine are not sprites: they are painted into the name table on their first step. The one in scene 1 comes down row by row to its place (p10:8E0D).
